feat: platformer damage system, enemy AI, and respawn-only-on-fall

## Damage & respawn (player / HealthManager / DeathZone)

- Respawn only when leaving the playable area: take_damage() no longer
  emits player_died; respawn is triggered only by request_respawn_from_fall().
- DeathZone (pits): calls request_respawn_from_fall() so falling into a pit
  costs one life and respawns at last checkpoint.
- Death/spike tiles (DEATH_TILES): touching these now calls
  request_respawn_from_fall() instead of take_damage(), so the player
  respawns at the last checkpoint and does not get stuck losing lives
  repeatedly. Added (1,0) to DEATH_TILES; list includes (0,1), (1,0), (1,1),
  (6,0), (6,1), (7,2).
- After any damage: 2s invulnerability and blink (platformer standard).
  Blink toggles sprite visibility every 0.08s during invulnerability.
- Enemies respect player i-frames: _damage_player() skips if
  get_damage_immunity_remaining_ms() > 0. Respawn grants brief invincibility.
- Non-fatal enemy hit: small knockback; fatal enemy hit: damage jump +
  death_tile animation before game over.
- HealthManager: damage_cooldown_ms 500; request_respawn_from_fall() for
  fall/death-tile respawn; at 0 lives fall triggers game_over().

## Enemy AI (Enemy.gd, states)

- State machine: Idle → Chase (in range) → Attack (in attack range); turn
  only at real walls and platform edges, not at ladders or collectibles.
- Single source of damage: only Attack state calls _damage_player() (one hit
  per attack); collision handles stomp only (no side-contact damage).
- Notify player before take_damage: on_damaged_by_enemy(pos) so death
  jump and death_tile animation work when killed by enemy.
- Dynamic movement: is_wall_ignorable() — do not turn when the only
  collision is player or collectible (enemy walks past when player moves).
- Turn-around cooldown (0.5s) and tighter lookahead (edge/obstacle offset
  4px) to avoid flip-flop and turn at the actual edge.
- Removed LADDER_TILES and ladder_or_obstacle_ahead(); enemy ignores
  ladders and gems for movement space.

## Collectibles

- Collectible.gd: add_to_group("collectible") so enemies ignore gems/keys
  when deciding to turn (is_wall_ignorable).
